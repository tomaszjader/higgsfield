# Tomasz Jąder — Cinematic Developer Portfolio
Design read: Independent Polish fullstack developer and AI experimenter, for prospective collaborators and curious technologists. Confidence, intelligence, warmth and personality rather than generic silicon-valley heroics.
Concept spine: **The human behind the systems.** Scroll begins with a documentary portrait becoming alive; the following project atlas shows the systems he makes. Material leitmotif: illuminated glass circuit, precision lines and numbers.
Delivery tier: cinema.
Palette: graphite #10151B, deep frame #0C1015, pearl #F5F2EB, cool fog #AEB8BF, persimmon #F1876C. Adapted from the original dark visual language, but evolves its formerly neon accent into a sophisticated warm human focal color. Restrained chrome.
Type: Space Grotesk for big editorial headlines, DM Sans for text and metadata, fallback sans-serif.
Animation mode: animated-website.
Journey shape: single-shot, one reference-driven portrait film, scroll plays the entire continuous personal gesture, no hard cuts.
Journey: chapter 1 — encounter, human face arrives from darkness, 'Tworzę technologie z ludzką perspektywą', copy emphasizing fullstack/AI. Journey expands into HTML chapters: manifest/tech stack, projects, AI experiments, beyond work, contact.
World grammar: authentic portrait of the site's real author in dark espresso top; black graphite studio, soft grey glass architecture, persimmon edge light, 50mm lens, lateral orbit push-in; subject center-safe and dark side negative space.
Mobile framing: portrait remains center safe, dark fade and title at bottom, mobile 720p H.264.
Delivery budget: desktop <=32 MiB, mobile <=16 MiB.
Section plan:
1. Fullscreen cinematic scroll portrait (immersive pinned hero)
2. Editorial manifesto with original portrait print, big statement (asymmetric split)
3. Stacked technology expertise (typographic index layout)
4. Horizontal-feature selected works, 2x2 grid and bespoke links (project cards)
5. AI lab editorial band with abstract generated background (image and overlay)
6. Personal four-tile photo story (masonry)
7. Contact curtain/large display CTA/footer.
Assets: original portrait, original logo, four original passion photos, original project/article images; generated architectural illustration and OG scene; encoded portrait film and exact first-frame posters; favicon from owner's original.
CTA inventory: header mail pill (tactile slide), hero anchor (underline arrow), each project row trailing circled arrow, lab repository text-link, email footer giant underline, socials minimal text.
Accessibility: semantic headings, reduced-motion media fallback, accessible anchors, robust contrast and keyboard focus.
SEO: document title, description, OG card, canonical links and favicon. Existing site is not modified.
